## Design Context

### Brand
**Nomio** — a collaborative travel journal built around an interactive map.

### Users
Travel groups (friends, couples, families) who want to collaboratively pin and revisit trip memories. Used primarily on mobile, often while traveling. Both authenticated users and guests participate.

### Brand Personality
Clean, warm, memorable. Not corporate. Feels like a well-designed travel journal — personal and trustworthy.

### Aesthetic Direction
**Clean & premium** — like Notion or Linear. Light mode. Lots of breathing room. Refined typography. Icons over emoji. Category colors as subtle tints, not heavy fills. No glassmorphism, no gradients on text, no side-stripe borders.

### Color Palette
- Primary: `#4f46e5` (indigo-600) — brand color
- Primary dark: `#4338ca` (indigo-700) — hover/active states
- Background: `#f6f6fe` — barely-there indigo tint on near-white
- Surface alt: `#f0f0fc` — indigo-tinted card surfaces
- Border: `#e4e4f0` — indigo-tinted neutral border
- Focus ring: `#a5b4fc` (indigo-300)
- Shadows use `rgba(79, 70, 229, ...)` for indigo-tinted depth

### Font
Figtree (Google Fonts, loaded via next/font) — warm geometric grotesque. Variable font with weights 400–800.

### Icon System
Font Awesome Free (NPM: @fortawesome/react-fontawesome). Category mapping in `lib/categories.ts`. Map pins use inline SVGs.

### Design Principles
1. **Breathing room** — generous padding, nothing cramped
2. **Color as signal** — category colors used sparingly as tinted backgrounds, not solid fills
3. **Hierarchy through weight** — bold titles, muted metadata, clear labels
4. **Icons earn their place** — FA icons replace all emoji in UI, map pins use clean inline SVG
5. **Interactions feel instant** — 0.12–0.18s transitions, hover states on every interactive surface
